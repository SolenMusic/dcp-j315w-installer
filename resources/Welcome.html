DCP-J315W Printing and Scanning

Wi-Fi printing and native Image Capture scanning for the Brother DCP-J315W, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

Choose Printing, Scanning, or both on the Installation Type page. Both are selected by default. Unchecking a component does not uninstall an existing installation.

Printing uses the standard macOS print dialogs. Scanning is configured for your account using DCP-J315W Setup, installed in Applications. The setup app also discovers your printer and offers component removal.

USB/direct wired printing and scanning are not implemented. Only the DCP-J315W is supported.

Created with LLM-assisted coding. I hope this keeps your printer and scanner useful for a while longer.
