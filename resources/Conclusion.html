Selected components installed

Open DCP-J315W Setup in Applications. Choose Find on Network, or enter the printer's IPv4 address, then Configure Selected.

Choose DCP-J315W CUPS Driver when printing and DCP-J315W Open Scanner in Image Capture. Printing may already be configured automatically if the printer was discoverable.

The setup app can remove either component. Printing removal requires administrator authentication. Scanning removal preserves saved scans and other users' configured helpers. Choose Remove Setup App after removing the driver components to remove the remaining setup files.
